About Alliance Healthcare

Delivering Trusted Pharmaceutical Care Since 2004

ALLIANCE Healthcare began its journey in 2004 with a clear vision to provide high-quality and affordable pharmaceutical solutions to patients across India.
Today, ALLIANCE Healthcare is a well-established and trusted name in the North Indian pharmaceutical market, with a strong presence in Gastroenterology, Medicine, and Surgery segments. The company’s brands are widely prescribed by leading doctors, reflecting consistent quality and clinical trust.
One of its flagship brands, VISAB, is among the most commonly prescribed laxatives by gastroenterologists, physicians, and surgeons across North India.
